# Demo and submission

## A single story

An indoor plant stylist publishes an office project, identifies her contribution, lists a consultation and shares her portfolio with a client. The intended complete payment demonstration is a client paying for that consultation through Lightning.

## Demo sequence

1. State the problem: work evidence is scattered and difficult to assess.
2. Show one portfolio and its project evidence.
3. Show the relationship between a skill and the project where it was used.
4. Show the service, rate and direct contact action.
5. Demonstrate Flash checkout only after it is implemented and tested. Until then, explicitly describe payment receiving details as display-only.
6. Explain user feedback, what changed and the next practical milestone.

Use the lash technician and fashion designer as brief examples of how the structure serves other professions.

## Honest feature claims

- Skill-to-project mapping is not independent credential verification.
- Portfolio publishing does not guarantee employment.
- Current Site access is private.
- M-Pesa/Lightning details do not currently process payments.
- AI assistance and starter/dependency licences should be acknowledged.
